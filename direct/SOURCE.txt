Direct browser port of id Software LinuxDOOM 1.10
=================================================

Engine/game source baseline:
https://github.com/id-Software/DOOM
pinned commit: a77dfb96cb91780ca334d0d4cfd86957558007e0

Browser platform replacement source and explicit compatibility edits:
https://github.com/pavy23/web-doom/tree/direct-linuxdoom/direct-port

Build system: Emscripten 6.0.5 + SDL2 + SDL2_mixer/WebAudio.
The original gameplay/rendering/game-state code remains id's LinuxDOOM;
platform i_video/i_system/i_sound/i_net are replaced for the browser,
plus narrowly scoped modern-toolchain compatibility edits documented in
direct-port/apply_compat.py.

Audio is implemented in this repository's direct-port/i_sound_web.c:
- DOOM DMX type-3 SFX lumps are parsed directly and converted to PCM/WAV.
- Original pitch variation is encoded into sample-rate conversion.
- DOOM stereo separation and volume are mapped to browser mixer channels.
- DOOM MUS music is converted by our code to Standard MIDI, then rendered
  by SDL2_mixer through the browser audio backend.

The included IWAD is the redistributable DOOM shareware data fetched
from SDL's long-standing DOOM project archive during CI.
