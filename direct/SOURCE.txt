Direct browser port of id Software LinuxDOOM 1.10
=================================================

Engine/game source baseline:
https://github.com/id-Software/DOOM
pinned commit: a77dfb96cb91780ca334d0d4cfd86957558007e0

Browser platform replacement source and explicit compatibility edits:
https://github.com/pavy23/web-doom/tree/direct-linuxdoom/direct-port

Build system: Emscripten 6.0.5 + SDL2 + SDL2_mixer + WebAudio.
The original gameplay/rendering/game-state code remains id's LinuxDOOM;
platform i_video/i_system/i_sound/i_net are replaced for the browser,
plus narrowly scoped modern-toolchain/platform edits documented in
direct-port/apply_compat.py.

Audio implementation:
- DOOM DMX type-3 SFX lumps are parsed directly and converted to PCM/WAV.
- Original SFX pitch variation, volume and stereo separation are preserved.
- DOOM MUS music is parsed directly at its native 140 Hz timing.
- The original IWAD GENMIDI lump supplies 128 melodic and 47 percussion
  instrument definitions to our repository-owned browser FM engine.
- GENMIDI modulator/carrier multiplier, level, ADSR-like rates, waveform,
  feedback/connection, fixed-note, base-note offset, fine tuning and
  double-voice fields drive WebAudio 2-operator FM graphs.
- The synth models the OPL2/AdLib nine-voice constraint with voice stealing.
- Music does not require Timidity, browser MIDI, or an external SoundFont.
- This is an OPL2-style reconstruction, not a cycle-accurate YM3812 emulator.

Music source:
direct-port/i_music_opl_bridge.c
direct-port/opl_music.js

The browser UI is repository-owned: direct-port/shell.html replaces
Emscripten's generated demo shell while Emscripten remains the C-to-WASM
build toolchain underneath. A user click starts the game and unlocks
browser audio according to autoplay policy.

The included IWAD is the redistributable DOOM shareware data fetched
from SDL's long-standing DOOM project archive during CI.
