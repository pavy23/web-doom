Reading the panel: advance / fight / retreat / dodge are the mode probabilities (the highlighted bar is the mode applied); fire is the shoot judgment; danger the danger score; target the target probabilities; a yellow badge is a code-owned safety rule overriding the model. How to run it yourself: README, Autoplay and mcp/AUTOPLAY.md.
The clips are VP8 in a WebM container, which every current browser plays. Slide and video editors often do not. To convert a downloaded clip to H.264 MP4 with any full ffmpeg build:

ffmpeg -i web-doom-e1m1-hmp-jev.webm -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 20 -pix_fmt yuv420p web-doom-e1m1-hmp-jev.mp4

The recorder itself only needs a VP8 encoder, which is why the build Playwright ships is enough to produce these.
